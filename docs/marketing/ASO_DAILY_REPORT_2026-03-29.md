## 🏆 Competitor Insights

### 1. MoodMusic: Daily Check-In
- **Positioning:** AI tells *you* what song to listen to (passive recommendation)
- **Gap for ONE:** ONE flips this — *you* choose the song, your taste defines your mood. This is more personal and authentic. Lean into "you choose, not AI" angle.
- **Their keyword**: `daily check-in`, `mood music`, `song recommendation`
- **Requires** iOS 18.2+ — ONE's iOS 16 target is a significant reach advantage

### 2. Harmonia — Music Mood Journal
- **Positioning:** AI playlists, Spotify-connected, focus/relaxation/mindfulness
- **Gap for ONE:** Harmonia is solo-focused with no social layer. ONE's Circle/Çevre friend-sharing is a completely unclaimed space.
- **Their keywords**: `music mood journal`, `AI playlists`, `relaxation`, `focus`
- **Avoid direct collision with:** `deep focus`, `relaxation`, `mindfulness` — these belong to Harmonia's semantic territory

### 3. Daylio / Moodnotes (general mood trackers)
- **Positioning:** CBT-based, journaling, habit tracking, mental health
- **Gap for ONE:** These are text/emoji-heavy. ONE is the only app making **music** the primary emotional input. Lead with this.
- **Insight:** Users searching `mood journal` or `mood diary` are open to music-based alternatives — ONE should appear in these searches via description copy even if not in keyword field.
